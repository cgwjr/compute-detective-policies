Compute Detective policies

Use Terms

Effective 15 October 2026. Provider and contact: Reddit user u/Ok-Worker-2717.

Compute Detective is a free research and comparison app. It displays time-stamped GPU rental price records and a limited set of model-token rate references. It does not rent servers, sell model tokens, reserve capacity, process payments or commit to a final bill. It is not a complete supplier catalog or real-time inventory service.

How to use price information

Prices may differ by account, region, term, usage, taxes and other cost components. Check a record's source, original currency and billing unit, cost scope, collection time and validity before comparison. A valid price does not establish current availability or purchase eligibility. Confirm specifications, availability and final payable amounts directly with the supplier before a purchase decision. Supplier websites and services have their own terms.

The app may omit records that are expired, unverifiable or outside its display scope. It may change or suspend a source when evidence or usage rights are insufficient. Supplier names identify the source of records; unless expressly stated, their appearance does not mean that a supplier sponsors, endorses or partners with Compute Detective or Reddit.
